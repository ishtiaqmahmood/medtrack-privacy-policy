Privacy Policy

How MedTrack handles your information

🛡️
MedTrack is a completely offline app. It does not collect or share any of your data.

☁No data collection

MedTrack collects nothing about you. There are no analytics or tracking SDKs, no usage telemetry, no crash reports with personal information, and no advertising identifiers. We do not ask for an account, an email address, or a phone number — the app works fully without ever identifying you.

⌁No internet, no transmission

The app has no internet connectivity at all. Every medication, dose record, journal entry, symptom note, doctor contact, and inventory count lives only in an encrypted database inside this device's storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server, because there is no server.

🔒No data sharing or selling

We never sell, rent, trade, or share your data — with advertisers, brokers, researchers, or anyone else. No third-party SDKs run inside the app that could exfiltrate information. Your health data stays on this device unless you deliberately export it yourself.

◷Reminders stay on your device

Medication reminders and alerts are scheduled locally through your operating system's own notification mechanism. They are not routed through any push-notification service, and no notification content leaves the device.

📁You control exports and backups

Exporting data — via the encrypted backup file, CSV export, PDF report, or the home-screen widget — happens only when you explicitly trigger it, and the result is stored or shared solely where you choose. Backups are encrypted with keys held in this device's secure keystore.

▤Deleting the app deletes everything

Because no copy of your data exists anywhere else, uninstalling MedTrack permanently removes every record it created (apart from any backup files you saved yourself, which you can delete at any time). There is no account to close and no server-side data to request removal of.

♧Children's privacy

MedTrack does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — including children — because it cannot collect personal information from anyone. Family profiles exist only locally on your own device.

▦Changes to this policy

This policy describes the app as it works today. If a future version were ever to change how data is handled, this screen would be updated first and the new effective date shown below.
